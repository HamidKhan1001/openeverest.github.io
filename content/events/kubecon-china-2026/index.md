## Project Lightning Talk

**Architecture Without Gatekeepers: Decoupling for Neutrality** — a Project
Lightning Talk by Sergey Pronin (Founder, Solanica).

Open source only works if the community can contribute without hitting a wall. We
realized our architecture at OpenEverest was unintentionally biased toward specific
tools, so we rebuilt it. Sergey will share how we moved to a modular plugin system
that decouples our core from specific operators. By supporting everything from
Valkey to competing Postgres flavors, we've eliminated vendor lock-in and ensured
the community — not the architecture — dictates the project's future.

→ [View the session details](https://kubecon-cloudnativecon-openinfra-pytorch-2026.sessionize.com/session/1223701)

## Why stop by the Project Pavilion?

OpenEverest is a CNCF Sandbox project for unified, vendor-neutral database
provisioning and management on Kubernetes. We'll be at the Project Pavilion on
Wednesday — here's what we'll be talking about:

- **OpenEverest v2 and new providers & plugins** — a deep dive into the v2 release,
  what changed under the hood, and the new database providers and plugin
  architecture that shipped with it.
  → [Read the v2 dev preview announcement](/blog/v2-developer-preview-release/)
- **LFX Mentorship** — the next term is opening up. If you're interested in
  contributing to an open-source CNCF project with real mentorship, come find out
  how to apply.
- **Data on Kubernetes community** — we'll share stats and insights on where the
  community is heading and what patterns we're seeing across production deployments.

We'll have stickers. Bring your questions — and your production war stories.
